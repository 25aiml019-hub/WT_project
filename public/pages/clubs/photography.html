About Photography Club

The CHARUSAT Photography Club is the creative lens of the university community. The club unites photographers, cinematographers, drone pilots, and graphic editors who document campus life, historic architecture, biodiversity of the Changa countryside, and cultural milestones.

Members learn foundational camera physics, shutter speed mechanics, depth of field, framing rules, natural lighting manipulation, and professional post-processing workflows using Adobe Lightroom and Photoshop.

Objectives & Core Activities

Visual Storytelling & Framing

Training sessions on composition rules, perspective, contrast, golden hour lighting, and documentary visual journalism.

Campus Event Photo Coverage

Official media coverage for university convocations, national conferences, sports leagues, and cultural festivals.

Weekend Photo Walks & Expeditions

Field trips to heritage sites, bird sanctuaries, rural landscapes, and architectural wonders across Gujarat.

Digital Darkroom & Retouching

Masterclasses in RAW image processing, color grading, exposure blending, HDR compilation, and print portfolio curation.

History & Background

Founded to celebrate visual storytelling, the Photography Club has maintained the photographic chronicles of CHARUSAT since its inception. The club's annual exhibitions during Tech Fest and cultural week draw hundreds of visitors from across academic institutions in Gujarat.

Administrative Archival Notice: Photo archives, contest awards from national photography salons, and high-resolution media galleries are archived by the University Media & PR Cell.

Flagship Media Events

  • Drishti (Annual National Photo Salon): Open competition attracting student entries in Portrait, Nature, and Creative categories.
  • Campus Through Your Lens (Monthly Theme Contest): Instagram and physical gallery showcases highlighting student perspectives.
  • Portraiture & Studio Lighting Intensive: Practical session with continuous studio lighting, softboxes, and reflectors.
  • Annual Heritage Photo Walk: Guided excursion documenting local stepwells, temple architecture, and historic towns.
