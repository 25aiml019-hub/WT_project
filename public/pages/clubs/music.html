About Music Club

The CHARUSAT Music Club serves as the creative haven for instrumentalists, vocalists, audio mixers, and rhythmists across all academic departments. From harmonizing soulful classical raagas to headlining high-octane rock performances during university youth festivals, the club nurtures diverse artistic expression.

The club is equipped with a soundproof rehearsal studio, standard acoustic drums, electric keyboards, guitars, tabla, amplifiers, and recording software to support budding musicians in original composition and arrangement.

Objectives & Core Activities

Vocal & Instrumental Training

Peer and master mentoring across Hindustani classical, Western vocal phrasing, guitar fingerpicking, and percussion.

Campus Jam Sessions (Unplugged)

Weekly open-mic acoustic evenings organized in the university amphitheater, encouraging casual musical expression.

University Fest Headliners

Composing original sets and battle-of-the-bands arrangements for Spoural and annual cultural celebrations.

Music Production & Mixing

Conducting workshops on digital audio workstations (DAWs), microphone acoustics, audio mastering, and studio recording.

History & Background

Formed by music enthusiasts in the early 2000s, the Music Club started as an informal acoustic circle beneath the campus neem trees. Over the years, with active institutional support from CHARUSAT Cultural Committee, it has evolved into a premier university ensemble with multiple specialized sub-bands representing the university at AIU National Youth Festivals.

Administrative Archival Notice: Discography archives, award listings from West Zone Youth Festivals, and previous batch leader rosters are curated by the Cultural Committee and updated each academic term.

Flagship Musical Events

  • Swaranjali (Annual Classical & Sufi Night): Evening of devotional, classical, and semi-classical performances held in the Central Auditorium.
  • Rhythm & Riffs (Battle of the Bands): High-voltage rock and contemporary band competition featuring campus groups.
  • Monsoon Acoustics: Casual open-air coffee-house acoustic sets during the monsoon semester.
  • Voice of CHARUSAT: University-wide solo and duet vocal competition judged by external classical musicians.
