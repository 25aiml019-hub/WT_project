About Coding Club

The CHARUSAT Coding Club is the central student developer community across Computer Engineering and Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning departments. The club's mission is to bridge theoretical computer science with real-world production engineering, preparing members for top-tier competitive hackathons, research publications, and software engineering careers.

From weekly algorithm sprint contests on platforms like LeetCode and Codeforces to building full-stack applications with modern stacks (React, Python, Node.js, PHP, MySQL, TensorFlow), members collaborate in project pods to solve meaningful campus and societal challenges.

Objectives & Core Activities

Competitive Programming Tracks

Structured problem-solving bootcamps covering dynamic programming, graph theory, trees, bit manipulation, and mathematical algorithms.

Hackathons & Prototype Sprints

Hosting 24-hour and 36-hour hackathons where interdisciplinary teams develop working AI, fintech, and campus automation prototypes.

Open Source & Git Mentorship

Guidance for contributions to global open-source ecosystems, Hacktoberfest participation, and GitHub portfolio architecture.

Tech Talks & Industry Seminars

Guest lectures and technical AMAs hosted by alumni software engineers from top tech companies and research institutes.

History & Background

Incepted by early computer science cohorts at CSPIT, the club has produced regional winners at national competitions including Smart India Hackathon (SIH), ACM ICPC regionals, and Google Summer of Code (GSoC) contributors. The club operates under peer-led leadership with mentorship from Department of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning faculty.

Administrative Archival Notice: Hackathon podium finishes, repository links for university-funded student patents, and alumni mentorship rosters are maintained by the departmental Innovation Cell.

Flagship Developer Events

  • CHARUSAT Annual Hackathon: 24-hour campus hackathon with over 50 teams building AI-powered web and mobile applications.
  • ByteCode (Inter-College Contest): Annual speed coding and algorithm tournament held in the university supercomputing cluster.
  • WebDev & Cloud Bootcamp: Hands-on technical series on modern web development, relational database modeling, and cloud deployments.
  • Open Source Sprints: Collaborative community sprint evenings focused on contributing to public open-source codebases.
